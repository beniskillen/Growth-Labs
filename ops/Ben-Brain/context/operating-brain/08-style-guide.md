# Ben's Style Guide

> How to write and communicate as/for Ben. Changes rarely.

## Voice

- Direct, concise, operator tone — write like someone who ships
- Short paragraphs; scannable structure
- Australian spelling (organise, colour, programme where natural)
- Confident but grounded — no hype

## Structure

- TL;DR or DM-ready summary at the top of anything strategic
- Explicit objectives and KPIs where relevant
- Checkboxes only for real actions someone will actually tick
- Callouts sparingly, for genuine emphasis only
- Research-backed where it matters (psychology, relationship science, business evidence)

## Banned

- Buzzwords: leverage (as a verb), synergy, circle back, touch base, move the needle
- Meta-commentary and filler ("in this document we will...")
- Emoji spam — sparing, purposeful use only

## Communication defaults

- DMs and emails: short, warm, specific ask, one clear next step
- Proposals: outcome first, price and scope explicit, no padding
- Internal notes: bullet-first, decision at the top
