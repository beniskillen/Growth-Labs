# Agreement architecture

Clone the structure of the house reference instrument (Growth Consultant Service Agreement: cover, At a Glance, parties, background recitals, numbered terms, schedules, execution). Restyle it in Growth Labs tokens. Do not flatten it into a 14-heading blog outline.

## Page spine

| Beat | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Cover | Identity, parties at a glance, REF |
| At a Glance | Six to eight rows a director can scan |
| Parties | Legal names, notices, joint definition of “party” |
| Background | Recitals A–D: who they are, why this exists, what is being recorded |
| Terms | Operative clauses, decimal numbering |
| Schedules | Facts that change per deal without rewriting doctrine |
| Execution | Signature blocks and onboarding leftovers |

## Cover

Dark ink field, paper type, 56px grid, signal radial in the upper right.

- Top-left: wordmark. Top-right: `SERVICE AGREEMENT` eyebrow.
- Kicker: `GROWTH LABS_ / SERVICES AGREEMENT` (or `/ PILOT`, `/ RETAINER`).
- H1: short commercial name (`Services Agreement`, not a slogan).
- Lede: one or two sentences — who pays whom, for what, for how long.
- Two party cards: **The provider** / **The client**.
- Foot: `REF … · VERSION … · MONTH YEAR · PRIVATE AND CONFIDENTIAL`.

## At a Glance rows (standard)

Use this set unless the brief truly has no analogue:

| Label | Typical content |
| --- | --- |
| What this is | Binding services contract. Not employment. Not an LOI. |
| Engaged by | Who invoices / who pays / remote or on-site |
| Term | Initial term and what happens at the end |
| Base fee | Amount, currency, cadence (in advance / on acceptance) |
| Commission | “None unless a separate attribution schedule is signed” by default |
| Included | One-line scope |
| Not included | Tools, production, paid media, extra brands, extra labour |
| Closes with | Review date, dashboard, go/no-go if any |

## Parties and background

Party (1) provider, party (2) client. Notices email. “Each a party and together the parties.” Effective date: last signature **or** a stated date — never invent it.

Recitals:

- **A** Client: what they do and the market that matters.
- **B** Provider: what they operate (systems, not vibes).
- **C** Relationship to Growth Labs / Ben if oversight is in play and they are **not** a signing party.
- **D** The parties now wish to record a paid engagement on the terms below.

## Standard clause map

Keep this order. Add a clause rather than stuffing a new topic into “Services” when it has its own commercial weight (SAM, founder layer, CRM, dashboard).

1. **Nature of this agreement** — binding services contract; independent contractor; no partnership/agency; what it replaces (unsigned drafts have no effect).
2. **Engaging entity and place of work** — who is engaged, remote/online, tax and permits are the provider’s affair unless stated.
3. **Term** — initial term; continuation requires a new writing.
4. **Objective and incorporated documents** — one objective paragraph; named briefs are incorporated; **this agreement controls on conflict once signed**.
5. **Services** — what is operated. Use `(a) (b) (c)`.
6. **Deliverables, baselines and goals** — point at Schedule 1; volumes are averages; not warranties.
7. **Fees and payment** — point at Schedule 2; pass-through costs; no extra compensation unless written.
8. **Time, reporting and working arrangements** — WIP, dashboard, hours if relevant.
9. **Authority** — neither party binds the other; outreach is subject to client approval.
10. **Roles and responsibilities** — provider / client / optional strategic oversight.
11. **Work product and intellectual property** — client-specific paid work vs pre-existing methods + internal-use licence.
12. **Confidentiality and data** — definition; no credentials in Notion; suppression/opt-out.
13. **Acceptance and change control** — five business days default; re-scope triggers.
14. **Termination** — breach + 10 business days to remedy; accrued fees; data return. Convenience terms stay `TO CONFIRM` until counsel/parties agree.
15. **General** — entire agreement stub; liability, indemnity, privacy, governing law, disputes, force majeure as `TO CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL`.

Optional extra clauses (insert after Services when the brief includes them):

- Serviceable addressable market and Dream Prospects.
- Founder and personal-brand layer (inclusions and hard exclusions).
- CRM and database reactivation.
- CEO Dashboard and key metrics (explicit non-guarantee).

## Schedules

| Schedule | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Deliverables, daily/weekly volumes, dashboard metrics, out of scope |
| 2 | Fee, billing, tools/pass-through, tax `TO CONFIRM`, change-order list, commission (or explicit none) |
| 3 | Open items: entities, addresses, effective date, convenience termination, governing law, signatories |
| 4 | Optional: Dream Prospect fields, carve-outs, claims boundary |

Every `TO CONFIRM` in the body **must** reappear in Schedule 3.

## Execution

Two columns. Legal name, human name, title, signature line, date. List anything to complete at onboarding underneath (SAM approval, account access, remaining legal facts).

## Mapping a 14-section outline onto this spine

If the user pastes a numbered brief (parties, objective, SAM, deliverables, … signatures):

- Cover + At a Glance absorb parties, term, fee, objective.
- Recitals absorb context that is not operative.
- Operative promises become clauses 4–15.
- Lists of fields, volumes, and fees become schedules.
- Signature blocks become Execution — do not leave a “§14 Signatures” heading in the terms.
