01 Nature of this agreement

1.2Nothing in this agreement creates an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture or agency. The Provider is engaged as an independent contractor.

1.3House letterhead and document design are Growth Labs. Unless Growth Labs is named as a signing party, it is not a party to this agreement.

02 Engaging entity and place of work

2.1The Provider is engaged by, invoices, and is paid by the Client only. {{PLACE_OF_WORK}}

2.2Each party is responsible for its own tax affairs, permits and authorisations in every jurisdiction in which it operates.

03 Term

3.1The initial term is {{INITIAL_TERM}} from {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} (the “Initial Term”), unless extended by written agreement or ended earlier under clause 14.

3.2Any continuation after the Initial Term requires a new written agreement. It does not arise automatically.

06 Deliverables, baselines and goals

6.1The Provider will produce the Deliverables in Schedule 1.

6.2Daily or weekly volumes in Schedule 1 are baselines, averaged across ordinary business days. Platform limits, deliverability, account health, public holidays, approval delays and list quality may require throttling.

6.3Goals and targets are planning tools. The Provider does not guarantee meetings, sales, revenue or specific platform performance. Failure to reach a target is not of itself a breach.

07 Fees and payment

7.1The Client will pay the fees in Schedule 2.

7.2Tools and pass-through costs are paid directly by the Client or reimbursed at cost with prior written approval.

7.3No commission, revenue share, bonus or equity is payable unless the parties sign a separate attribution schedule defining percentage, baseline, eligible opportunities, payment event, exclusions, refunds and tail period.

09 Authority

9.1Neither party may bind the other. The Provider may not sign contracts, vary pricing or make binding representations on the Client’s behalf without prior written approval.

9.2Where the Provider represents the Client in outreach, it will make clear that terms are subject to the Client’s approval.

11 Work product and intellectual property

11.1The Client owns its data, credentials, approved campaign records and final paid client-specific deliverables.

11.2The Provider retains pre-existing templates, methods, prompts and reusable operating systems. It grants the Client a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use the final materials paid for, for the Client’s internal business purposes.

11.3No credentials are stored in Notion. Access uses official sharing or OAuth methods.

12 Confidentiality and data

12.1Each party protects non-public business, client and prospect information of the other.

12.2The Client confirms it has the right to use every record it supplies and owns source accuracy, consent, exclusions and legal compliance. The Provider will not contact suppressed, risky or unapproved records.

13 Acceptance and change control

13.1A deliverable is accepted when it meets the written scope and the Client does not identify a material defect within five (5) business days.

13.2A request that changes channel count, market, daily volume, creative production, technical build or response workload requires written re-scoping before the extra work starts.

14 Termination

14.1Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within ten (10) business days of written notice. Fees accrued and approved pass-through costs remain payable.

14.2On termination the Provider will return or export agreed client data and revoke access within a reasonable transition period.

14.3A convenience-termination right, notice period and treatment of prepaid fees are TO CONFIRM before signature.

15 General

15.1Independent contractor relationship. Neither party may bind the other.

15.2Liability cap, indemnities, privacy terms, governing law, dispute process and force majeure are TO CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL.
