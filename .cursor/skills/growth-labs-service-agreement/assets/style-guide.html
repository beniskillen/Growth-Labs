How the pages stack

The house reference agreement is a 17-page services instrument. New deals reuse that hierarchy and swap Growth Labs visual language in. They do not become a fourteen-heading outline.

CoverDark field, wordmark, title, one-sentence lede, two party cards, REF line.
At a GlanceWhat this is, who engages whom, term, fee, commission (or none), inclusions, exclusions, how it closes.
PartiesLegal names, notices, “each a party”.
BackgroundRecitals A–D. Context, not operative promises.
TermsDecimal clauses 1.1 and lettered sub-clauses (a).
SchedulesVolumes, fees, open items, optional field lists.
ExecutionTwo signature columns. Onboarding leftovers listed underneath.

01 Clause typography

1.1Clause titles sit on a hairline with a signal index. Body uses a hanging number in Geist Mono. This is the only numbering system in the terms.

1.2House letterhead is Growth Labs. Contracting parties are whoever invoices and pays. Oversight that is not signing stays in a roles clause, not on the cover as a third party.

02 Open items

2.1Unresolved legal facts use TO CONFIRM. Counsel-only items use TO CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL. Every chip also appears in Schedule 3.

Not legal advice. This system produces a commercial draft. Liability, indemnity, privacy, governing law, disputes and force majeure are not filled from templates found on the internet.

How to produce a deal

  1. Copy assets/template.html and keep agreement.css.
  2. Fill parties, term, fee, scope and exclusions from the brief. Leave legal gaps marked.
  3. Map a pasted outline onto the spine: glance and recitals first, then clauses, then schedules, then execution.
  4. Render A4 PDF with scripts/render.mjs (Chrome, no browser header/footer).
  5. If the git remote is public, commit only the skill and blanks. Put the filled instrument under the client page in Notion.

Reference codes

GL-<PROVIDER>-<CLIENT>-<YEAR>-<NNN> — uppercase ASCII, short hyphenated names. Example: GL-NARA-SPECTRE-2026-001.
