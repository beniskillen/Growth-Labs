# Research playbook

Each module is an owned research job. Cite sources. Hoped numbers do not enter a deck.

**Good enough to pitch:** sourced, segmented, and tied to a decision.

**Do not put in a deck:** unsourced TAM, hoped impressions, competitor gossip, planning-case rates presented as forecasts.

Search **Notion first** (and Slack/Drive if connected), then the public web. One question per search call.

## 0. Existing architecture

**Queries:** client name, event name, sponsorship, COGS, top 100, funnel, waitlist.

**Evidence:** page URLs, last-edited dates, contradictions.

**Output:** inventory of pages to **reuse**. Do not duplicate.

## 1. Vision and north-star math

**Queries:** vision, mission, ticket price, attendance, backend price, sponsor target, net revenue.

**Evidence:** stated numbers vs modelled numbers vs evidenced numbers — three columns.

**Output:** work-back: tickets × price, backend × enrolments, sponsor cash, COGS band, net. Label planning-case.

**Deck test:** three numbers max on the cover.

## 2. Competitive and analogue map

**Queries:** category + geography + format (e.g. “Bali AI education event”, “operator cohort $12k”, “workshop conference”). Analogues the brief names (Hormozi launches, Gadzhi, Mastering the Markets, Tony Robbins pre-work, Priestley/Dent, Genius, Mindvalley) — copy **mechanisms**, not brands.

**Evidence:** price, fill mechanism, owned-list vs ads, sponsor type.

**Output:** who is preeminent at which **job to be done**. White space in one sentence. Fantasy competitors (enterprise AI summits, Nvidia cold) parked.

**Deck test:** one category claim. Not “best AI education company in the world” unless that job is actually ownable.

## 3. Audience and owned distribution

**Queries:** list sources (CRM, email, IG, ManyChat, alumni, Substack), mix (SMB / corporate / consumer), geos.

**Evidence:** counts, recency, consent, % with company/role.

**Output:** what a sponsor will ask (distribution, ICP, geos). Data gaps named.

**Deck test:** mix as ranges with source (“cohort observation”, “GHL export”), not fake precision.

## 4. Funnel constraint (valence)

**Queries:** impressions, unique views, CTR, landing CVR, bookings, show-up, close, DM volume.

**Evidence:** dashboard or dated Slack/Notion snapshot. Sample size.

**Output:** one named bottleneck. Current vs future path.

**Deck test:** if bookings are 0% on enough sample, that is the cover constraint. Stop.

## 5. Impression inventory

Itemise: paid, organic (IG/YT/LI/Substack), clipping, streaming, affiliates, partner swaps, speaker audiences, owned list sends.

**Evidence:** trailing 30/90 days where possible; event-day as a **separate** column (not mixed into “we have”).

**Output:** low / base / stretch. Hoped stays hoped.

## 6. Media value

`impressions × CPM band` with sources. Earned vs paid vs hoped.

**Deck test:** no single vanity total.

## 7. Sponsor first principles

For each **category** in the top 10: CAC analogue, ads model, what “yes” looks like (cash, credits, exclusivity, GTM-seat substitute).

**Queries:** “[brand] sponsorship”, “GTM engineer”, affiliate terms, event partnerships.

**Output:** chain reach → segments → attribution → conversion → LTV → ROI **in their units**.

## 8. Custom integration theses (top 10 only)

For each flagship: category job, agenda/coursework moments, attribution, fulfilment after event, downsell tier, warm route, decision-maker, ask, next date.

**Deck test:** one issued thesis in 30 days unless capacity says otherwise. The rest stay pipeline.

## 9. COGS and cash gates

Low / target / high. What cash must land before venue, speakers, production. Deposits. Entity who invoices.

**Evidence:** quotes > analogue > AI estimate (label the last).

**Deck test:** no commitment without a matching cash gate.

## 10. Ascension / LTV

Lead magnet → tripwire → ticket → discovery → programme / B2B / continuity.

**Evidence:** historic close rates if any. Otherwise planning-case with a 30-day measurement gate.

## 11. Channel systems vs lead-getter checklist

See hormozi-fladlien.md. Each getter In 30 / In 90 / Not now / Out + owner.

## 12. Workback inputs (not a substitute for 30/90)

Flagship date, lock-in dates, working days, conversation maths, hire gates. Feed [operating-cadence.md](operating-cadence.md).

## Research quality bar

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Round millions with no source | `Data gap` or drop |
| Competitor “they do $20m” with no evidence | Analogous, not a fact |
| “We will get 300M impressions” | Inventory first; stretch labelled stretch |
| Backend 10% take-up | Planning-case; measure first 20 calls |

## Tools

- Notion `search` then `fetch`
- Web search / fetch for public comps, CPM, sponsor GTM
- Slack search if connected (funnel snapshots, open asks)
- Never invent a dashboard number
