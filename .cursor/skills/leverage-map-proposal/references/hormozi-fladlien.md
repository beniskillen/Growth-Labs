# Hormozi / Fladlien bar

Encode **operating moves**, not book reports. If the artefact would embarrass a direct-response operator, rewrite it.

## Hormozi moves

### Name the constraint, then only work that constraint

Leads vs sales vs fulfilment. Prescribe more traffic only when conversion and fulfilment can hold it. “The person who spends the most on ads wins” is true **after** CAC sits under LTV with contribution margin — never as a spend brief while booking rate is zero.

### Value equation (every offer)

`(Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood of achievement) / (Time delay × Effort and sacrifice)`

If likelihood or speed is weak, specify the **proof asset** or **lead magnet** that fixes it. Do not raise the price. Do not add adjectives.

### Three offers, never one blob

Mixing ticket, backend, and sponsor packages is how proposals become unreadable.

1. **Client offer** — Growth Labs scope this cycle
2. **Buyer offer** — attendee / customer purchase
3. **Sponsor offer** — category job

Grand slam vs commodity: the flagship custom sponsor thesis is grand slam. Logo tiers are commodity downsells.

### More / Better / New

- **0–30 days:** more of the working motion, or better conversion of **owned** demand
- **31–90 days:** a **new** channel only after the 30-day scoreboard moved
- Never invent twelve channels in week one

### Lead getters (checklist, not a chapter summary)

For each, mark **In 30 / In 90 / Not now / Out** with a reason:

1. Warm outreach (owned graph)
2. Content / posting
3. Customer referrals
4. Affiliates / JVs / speakers as distribution
5. Employees / partners
6. Agencies
7. Cold outreach
8. Paid ads (multiplier, not mother engine)

Warm graph first. Matches Growth Labs: owned demand is the cheapest high-intent traffic they will ever have.

### Money model, not a revenue wish

Attraction offer → upsell → downsell → continuity.

Show **cash this month**, **contribution margin**, and **continuity**. Unproven take-up = **planning-case**. Do not starve operations for vanity LTV.

Example (planning-case only): 1,000 × $499 tickets is not the business if the doctrine is 1,000 discovery calls that ascend into a $12k programme and a services arm. Model call capacity or refuse the doctrine.

### Fulfilment before scale

If every ticket is a 1:1 enterprise conversation, show closer-days. Meter supply to call capacity. A 1,000-seat promise with two founders on the phone is a fulfilment constraint pretending to be a marketing plan.

## Fladlien moves

### Unique mechanism

Name it in one line. For Growth Labs:

> **Leverage Map + valence scoreboard** — impressions → unique views → CTR → CAC → LTV → page CVR, with TAM as the outer bound — so the result feels inevitable rather than “we’ll do marketing”.

The client is not buying activity. They are buying a machine with a governor.

### Offer clinic

Ask: **what would have to be true for yes to be stupid to refuse?**

Stack **only** bonuses that kill a real objection (proof, speed, risk reversal, fulfilment, make-goods). No fake timers. No 17-item bonus sludge.

### Demo in the pitch

The Notion architecture and FigJam board **are** the demo. Do not describe “we are thorough” in prose if the artefact can show the machine.

### One primary CTA

Approve the **30-day bet** and appoint or cut **UNASSIGNED** seats. Everything else is appendix.

### Incrementality

One lead-magnet test, one booking-path test, one flagship sponsor thesis. Not twelve simultaneous inventions.

### Reason why

Date, price, and “why these 30 days” need a reason why. April vs October is a **what would have to be true** table (cash, demand, production, speakers, conversion proof) — not a vibe.

## Proof stack

Every shareable number needs one of: owned dashboard, quote, public analogue with URL, or `Data gap`.

Specific beats claimed. “113 unique views, 0 bookings” beats “we have a strong community”.

Heritage numbers (Juicy, CryptoVega, Bybit) prove **Growth Labs has run this method**. They do not prove the current client’s reach.

## Palatability (the Loom is not the deck)

Operator download can be 80 minutes. Client artefact is four minutes on the cover.

Kill list for shareable packs:

- Personal history, trust narratives, undeclared hour commitments
- Fantasy logos with no warm route
- Impression totals that are hoped
- Backend conversion rates presented as forecasts
- Workstreams with no owner
