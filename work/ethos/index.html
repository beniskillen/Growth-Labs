THE DIAGNOSIS

The constraint was the offer. Then the system that could sell it when I was not in the room.

Part-time seats only work if the thing you install does not need you to perform it. We separated floor revenue from the outcome people would pay more for, and we refused to let those numbers share a sentence.

01

Advertising leak — talking like a gym

General fitness traffic is the wrong room. Combat performance buyers are a smaller list and a much clearer one.

Audience was whoever walked past
02

Interest leak — a timetable is not interesting

People already know what a membership is. They do not stop for it. They stop for a result they recognise as theirs.

Nothing specific to click
03

04

THE PROCESS

How the category actually got built

This is a career seat, 2021 to 2023, part-time. I owned marketing and sales strategy around the new offer. I did not own the mats.

  1. 01
    Step 01 — Separate the money

    Floor hours are not the category

    If you cannot see the new line on its own, you will congratulate yourself for a busy gym. We pulled the outcome out of the timetable before we tried to market it.

  2. 02
    Step 02 — Productise it

    Something a stranger can buy

    High margin, online, specific to combat performance. The floor stayed. The product meant the result did not require another booked hour to exist.

  3. 03
    The stack

    Site, content, traffic, BD

    One story. Not a class timetable with ads on top. The site had to say the outcome in the first screen or the traffic was wasted.

  4. 04
    The number

    Keep the $80k honest

    That figure is the new line, first 12 months. It is not the gym’s total revenue. I keep saying that because blended numbers are how these stories go rotten.

  5. THEN
    Then

    The floor is the craft. The category is the business.

    The positioning I will use is combat performance, not “we are the number one gym in the country.” A ranking without a source is a slogan. The $80k is a source.
