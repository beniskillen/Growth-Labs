THE CHALLENGE

A great studio, invisible to the buyer who would actually hire it.

If you have ever run commercial inside a creative business, you know this feeling. The work is good. The ads are not getting the impressions. And when they do get seen, the click-through is not there. Internally that gets read as “the creative is not working.” It usually is not the craft. It is that nothing in the market has a reason to stop.

Fortem was a full menu. Video, social, brand, strategy. Menus get compared on price. The buyer we wanted was a brand that needed to own a culture, not rent another asset. They were not in the feed in any density that mattered.

THE DIAGNOSIS

This was not a media problem. It was a category problem wearing a media costume.

The obvious move was a better media plan. I did not start there. A generalist has to win every impression from scratch, and the auction taxes that. Low impressions and a weak click were the scoreboard. We had not given the market one thing to be the obvious choice for.

THE PROCESS

How the category actually got built

I owned the commercial diagnosis and the pipeline. Fortem owned the making, and the build of the club. This is the order it actually ran.

  1. 01
    Step 01 — Get clear on what’s really going on

    Name the constraint. It was attention, not talent.

    We were not getting enough impressions, and the click-through was not high enough. That is a painful thing to say inside a creative company, because everyone hears it as a judgement on the work. It was not. It was a judgement on the category.

    Once that was named, the job stopped being “make the ads better” and became “give the ideal buyer a reason to already be in the room.”

  2. 02
    Step 02 — Reach them in a novel way

    Unofficial Run Club, as the category asset

    We did not sell run-club marketing. We built the club. A weekly ritual, a name, a standard. It started at basically nothing, about four followers, no sponsors, no leverage.

    Social was the record of the room, not a content calendar hoping for reach. Followers went from about 4 to 64k. About 1,000 people a week turned up. That is impressions you own. New Balance and Whoop came toward the culture. So did other sport brands. They were not buying a media kit. They were asking to be let into a room that was already full. That is earned media. That is the whole move.

  3. 03
    The offer that can scale

    One mechanism a brand can buy

    The club made the sentence true: we do not only ship the asset, we build the place brands pay to enter. The commercial offer around that was culture-led brand growth. Amplify the strategy a brand already has. Do not replace their team.

    Scala Lane sat next to it as the click receipt. When the angle was specific, the click-through was 9%, more than 300% above a typical property benchmark around 2 to 2.5%. Sharp creative earns the click. The club earns the fact you get seen at all.

  4. 04
    Steps 03–04 — Then run it as a system

    Stop restarting the pitch

    With the category named, business development stopped being a fresh performance every week. Paid into a named offer. Cold sequences. The warm graph. One proof stack a buyer could understand in a single sitting.

    I ran that conveyor. The studio kept making the work. Those are different jobs, and the case only works if you keep them separate.

  5. THEN
    Then — keep what works

    The room kept pulling

    Unofficial Run Club travelled. Sydney, then further, including New York. The commercial number attached to the seat is $1.5M+ in sales. The club is still the thing you can visit. That is how you know the category held.
