THE CHALLENGE

Speed and fees are how recruiters disappear into each other.

Frontier teams hire people who understand the work. A firm that says “we fill roles” is interchangeable, and interchangeable gets squeezed on the fee.

There is also no way to tell a busy week from a business if you cannot see emails becoming calls becoming paid searches.

THE DIAGNOSIS

The market is specific enough to own. The constraint is category and cadence.

I would rather a blunt funnel than a brand film. If the lane is real, the ratios will tell us. If the lane is a costume, the ratios will tell us that too, faster than a mood board will.

THE PROCESS

How the category actually got built

I design the commercial system. Jobited owns the talent and the delivery. The ratios are the design.

  1. 01
    Step 01

    Refuse the generic recruiter frame

    Frontier hiring intelligence. If we cannot say that without wincing, we are not ready to email anyone.

  2. 02
    Step 02

    Write the funnel down

    About 1,000 employer emails a month, roughly 20 calls, roughly 2 paid outcomes. A model with owners. Now we can see which step is lying.

  3. 03
    The rooms

    Go where the category is already obvious

    Partnerships, incubators, hackathons. Those are not a brand exercise. They are where this buyer already stands. Content has to sound like the work.

  4. 04
    The week

    A rhythm, or it is a hobby

    KPIs, an offer, a weekly operating rhythm. Change the broken ratio. Do not add a channel because the first one feels slow.

  5. THEN
    Then

    Update this page when the paid outcomes are real

    Until then the honest result is a firm that can be the obvious choice for one kind of hiring, with a machine that can prove it. The $30k is the destination on the map.
