THE CHALLENGE

Property advertising is a sea of the same render.

Same building language, same “register your interest,” same auction. Click-through collapses because nothing in the ad is a point of view. People are not ignoring property. They are ignoring ads that could be swapped onto the building next door.

That was the same shape as Fortem’s wider problem. The click was not high enough to be the argument. One sharp campaign does not fix an agency. It proves the agency can win the click when the angle is real.
