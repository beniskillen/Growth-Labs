Category King · Property

A sharp sentence is not a category until the client experience matches it.

From November 2022 to May 2023 I was Head of Growth at The Investors Agency. The firm already had the right ambition: financial freedom through investment-grade property, not “we are a buyers agency.” My job was customer success and the growth system around that promise. A headline that delivery does not keep is just copy. I have written enough of that to know.

SNAPSHOT

Before the category, the work had to be pushed.

Buyers agencies blur. Same suburbs, same negotiation story, same portal. A firm can have done an enormous amount of work and still sound generic in the first five minutes. The category sentence was the advantage. The leak was whether the operation carried it.

THE CHALLENGE

If you promise freedom and then hand someone a generic purchase, they will not repeat you.

The market of buyers agencies is crowded with competent people. Competence is not a category. The Investors Agency had a sharper outcome than “we find property.” The risk was that the outcome lived on the website and the week looked like every other brokerage.

Growth, in that seat, was not more ads. It was whether a client could feel the promise after they had said yes.

THE DIAGNOSIS

The constraint was whether customer success could carry the category.

I looked at the promise and then at the path. Onboarding, success, retention. If those do not use the same language as the headline, you do not own a category. You own a campaign.

THE PROCESS

How the category actually got built

Six months. I will not narrate it as a multi-year epic. The install is the case.

  1. 01
    Step 01

    Separate the category from the commodity

    “Buyers agency” is the commodity. Financial freedom via investment-grade property is the category. We used the second one.

  2. 02
    Step 02

    Put a system behind the yes

    Customer success, so the promise had an owner after the sale. Growth systems, so it was not a founder remembering to check in.

  3. 03
    The voice

    One sentence, including in the follow-up

    Content and warm outreach had to sound like the promise. A second brand voice is how categories fall apart.

  4. 04
    The boundary

    Their book of deals stays theirs

    The platform has a serious history of transactions. That is company context. It is not a number I produced, and I will not let a deck imply that I did.

  5. THEN
    Then

    A short seat can still be a true one

    If you tell the truth about scope, six months is enough to show the pattern: name the outcome, install the success system, do not borrow the firm’s trophies.
