THE CHALLENGE

More content will not turn three projects into one place.

Operators do not need another network. They need a room with a standard. If I cannot say what that standard is, I should not be inviting anyone.

The other failure mode is me. An ecosystem that only works while I personally host it is not a category. It is a job I gave a nicer name.

THE DIAGNOSIS

A generalist community re-earns attention every week. A category gets pulled.

This is the same diagnosis I use on clients, pointed at myself. If the room does not have an identity, every Thursday is a new sale. Partners cannot buy a calendar. They can buy a specific kind of operator, gathered on purpose.

How the category actually got built

  2. 02
