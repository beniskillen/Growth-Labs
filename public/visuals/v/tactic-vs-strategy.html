The way most owners think about growth goes against the natural laws of the universe

This natural law is the Pareto distribution. Natural selection, and the resources of the many accruing to the few. Otherwise known as the 80/20 phenomenon, we see it applied to different ways of thinking, which highlights the major difference between unicorn growth tactics implemented by the top companies in the world and what most businesses do. Same two years. Same linear scale, up to $10M a year. One path copies the market. The other chooses where every resource goes.

Tactic-led

Doing what everyone else does

The same channels, campaigns and hires as every other owner.

Strategy-led

Every resource on the most valuable option

Unlimited options. One is clearly worth more. The whole business moves there.

Where the effort goes

Effort spread across the moves everyone else is already making. Small gains, flat months when a tactic stops working, and dips when it costs more than it returns. After two years the business is still about $3M a year.
